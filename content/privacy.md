<p>This policy explains what information HandyTaskLab collects for the Plex remote troubleshooting service, why it is used, and how to contact us.</p>

<h2>Information we collect</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Contact details you submit: name, email address, and message.</li>
  <li>Technical details needed for diagnosis: Plex host OS, client device, error messages, network symptoms, screenshots you choose to send, and what you have tried.</li>
  <li>Quote, payment, and scheduling records. Card data is entered on Creem's checkout and is not stored by HandyTaskLab.</li>
  <li>Session notes, test results, and written handover notes.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How we use it</h2>
<ul>
  <li>To answer your request, confirm scope, and prepare a written quote.</li>
  <li>To deliver and document the remote session and any approved changes.</li>
  <li>To process payment, refunds, and accounting through Creem.</li>
  <li>To protect the service against fraud, abuse, or unlawful requests.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Sharing</h2>
<p>We do not sell your information. We share only what is needed with service providers that operate the request form, email, hosting, and payment processing. Creem acts as merchant of record for payments and handles payment data under its own privacy terms. We may disclose information if required by law.</p>

<h2>Retention</h2>
<p>Request and session records are kept only as long as needed to deliver the service, handle a follow-up, meet accounting or legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Screenshots or recordings are kept only when needed for the agreed handover and are deleted when no longer required.</p>

<h2>Your choices</h2>
<p>You may ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information, subject to legal and accounting retention requirements. Do not send passwords. Redact account details and IP addresses from screenshots where they are not needed for diagnosis.</p>

<h2>Cookies and third parties</h2>
<p>This site uses a Google Forms request form and links to Creem for payment. Those services may process technical data under their own policies. This site does not use advertising cookies.</p>
