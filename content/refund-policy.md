<p>This policy applies to paid Plex remote troubleshooting sessions. It should be read with the <a href="/terms/">Terms of Service</a>.</p>

<h2>Before the session</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before the agreed start time for a full refund or one free reschedule.</li>
  <li>Cancel or reschedule less than 24 hours before the start time: one reschedule is allowed, subject to availability; a refund is at our discretion.</li>
  <li>If HandyTaskLab cancels or cannot deliver the session, you receive a full refund of the amount paid for that session.</li>
</ul>

<h2>After the session</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Once the remote session has been delivered and the written handover has been sent, the service is considered performed. Refunds are not automatic because the technical outcome depends on your ISP, router, hardware, and client.</li>
  <li>If the session could not be performed because of a fault on our side, we will offer a replacement session or a full refund.</li>
  <li>If you stop the session after work has started, the amount paid is non-refundable, but we will provide the findings gathered up to that point.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How refunds are issued</h2>
<p>Approved refunds are returned through Creem to the original payment method. Creem is the merchant of record and controls the payment and refund processing. Processing time depends on Creem and your bank or card issuer.</p>

<h2>Chargebacks</h2>
<p>Please contact <a href="mailto:hello@handytasklab.com">hello@handytasklab.com</a> before opening a chargeback so we can review the session record and resolve the issue directly.</p>

<h2>Contact</h2>
<p>For a cancellation or refund request, reply to your quote email or write to <a href="mailto:hello@handytasklab.com">hello@handytasklab.com</a> with the quote or order ID.</p>
